# Playing NumberTown

[Launch the game](../README.md#run-locally), then choose a friend and explore. The game has 51 characters numbered **0–50**. Choosing a wandering friend gives you control at their current location.

## Controls

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD or arrow keys | Walk relative to the camera view |
| Shift + movement | Walk faster |
| Drag the world / Q and R | Orbit the camera; vertical dragging changes elevation |
| Mouse wheel / + and − / zoom buttons | Zoom |
| C | Reset the camera |
| Space / Hop button | Hop on land; hold Space aboard the train for 2× speed |
| Z / X | Swim upward / downward; release to hold height |
| Esc | Leave train or stop garden tour |
| F / activity button | Kick football, pick up / throw beach ball, or board a nearby train |
| E / feeling menu | Change expression |
| T / Change shape | Cycle funny shapes, rectangles, irregular arrangements and a crazy shape, then return to the original |
| Shift + T / dice button | Generate a fresh connected crazy shape |
| K / Tell me a math fact | Ask the selected friend for another number fact |
| M / Map button | Open or close the town map |
| 1–9 and 0 | Choose One through Ten; the single 0 key chooses Ten |
| Shift + 1–9 and 0 | Choose Eleven through Twenty |
| Tab, number, Enter | Choose any friend from Zero through Fifty |
| Hold Tab, type a number, release Tab | Choose any friend with a held shortcut |
| Number field + Go | Choose any friend using the field |
| Thumbnails / previous and next buttons / [ and ] | Choose a friend from the full cast |
| Click a character in town | Control that character |
| Place buttons / map destinations | Travel to a place |
| Follow trail / Walk down | Start a guided mountain ascent or descent |
| Pause walk | Stop the guided walk |
| View sea / View town | Frame the view from a scenic deck |
| Tour garden / Stop tour | Follow or stop the seabed loop |

The bottom strip scrolls through all 51 thumbnails. To choose Zero directly, use **Tab + 0** or Zero’s thumbnail. **Escape** cancels number entry. Tab opens the number field when the game has focus; in menus, dialogs, and other controls, Tab and Shift + Tab keep their normal focus behavior.

On touch screens, use the direction pad, Hop, and the nearby activity buttons for kicking, picking up, and throwing. Drag with one finger to orbit and pinch with two fingers to zoom.

Movement keys or the direction pad immediately take over from a guided walk. Opening Help or the map, choosing another friend, or leaving the window stops the guide and clears held movement. Choose Follow trail or Walk down again to continue.

Friends you take onto Sunny Hill or the mountain route **stay where you leave them**, including both viewing decks, when you choose another character. Camera movement, shape changes, and looking around do not send them downhill. Select that friend again and use WASD, **Walk down** on the mountain, or a destination button to leave. Once back on flat ground, the friend can wander normally when unselected.

## Places and activities

The world spans **1,360 × 780 units**, with **80 houses**: 60 on the west bank and 20 on the east bank. There are also **30 stores**: 20 west and 10 east, and **3 public buildings**: the library, school, and town hall. Use the map to find destinations on both banks and in the expanded southern sea.

- **Town square:** a colorful Rubik’s cube statue balances on one corner and spins at 50 degrees per second, completing a turn every 7.2 seconds.
- **Duck pond and gardens:** follow the shore, watch the ducks, and explore the flowers and paths.
- **Ferris wheel park:** the east-bank wheel has 18 colorful cabins that remain upright as it turns. Walk through the park and picnic area.
- **Football:** visit the east-bank pitch, walk into the ball to dribble, and press F or Kick for a stronger shot. The whole ball must cross the line between the posts to score. It pauses briefly after a goal, then returns to the center with the score preserved. Shots outside the goal mouth bounce back. Reset ball also returns it to the center without clearing the score.
- **Sunny Hill:** follow the winding trail to the 12-metre hilltop and viewing deck. View sea frames the southern beach and water; drag to look around freely.
- **River and bridges:** the river runs continuously from the northern mountain gorge to the sea. The North, Market, and Coast bridges join the two neighborhoods. Characters follow the heights of bridges and hills as they walk.
- **Mountain summit:** visit Mountain trail and follow the paved bends manually or choose Follow trail. A progress bar tracks the ascent to the 72-metre summit deck. Walk down guides the return. This marked route reaches one summit; the other mountain slopes remain blocked. Sunny Hill is a separate, lower climb.
- **Beach and sea:** choose Seaside beach, then **Water play**, or select Water play on the map. The sea covers **194 × 262 units**, about eleven times the previous play area. The sand slopes continuously down to **18 blocks deep**. Use **WASD** to move horizontally, **Z** to rise and **X** to descend. Releasing Z and X holds your absolute height, even as you move over the sloping sand. The seabed and surface limit your movement; friends shorter than 15 blocks can go completely underwater, including their number labels. Drag to look around and zoom in for an underwater view. **Explore seabed**, or **Underwater garden** on the map, takes you directly to the deep sand. **Back to beach** returns you to shore.
- **Underwater garden:** explore colorful seaweed, tall kelp, rocks, branching corals, sea urchins and crabs around a small paved seabed loop. **Tour garden** lowers you to the path and guides you around it; WASD, Z/X, Esc or Stop tour returns control to you.
- **Discovery Train Park:** a new park east of town has desert, rainforest, snow mountains and farms, each with a few animals. Use Train park or the map, then **F / Board train** near the train. Your character rides inside and their body is hidden. The train moves automatically while occupied. **Hold Space** for exactly twice the speed and release it for normal speed. **Esc / Leave train** steps off beside the track. Switching friends or visiting another place also disembarks the rider and stops the train. **Find train** takes you to wherever it is parked.
- **Animal encounters:** your selected character shares a short fact when near an animal, including train-park animals, sea life and pond ducks. Animals at a different water depth must also be nearby vertically. Facts have a pause between them, and wandering friends remain quiet. Optional voices speak the same fact shown in the bubble.
- **Sea life:** find schools of colorful fish and five-armed sea stars on the sandy floor. Sea turtles swim through the bay, crawl onto the beach to bask, then return to the water. The clear surface lets you see below from above; an underwater camera gets a blue-green view.
- **Beach ball:** walk close to the striped ball at the same depth and press **F / Pick up**, face your throwing direction with WASD, then press **F / Throw**. Carry it below the surface using X for an underwater throw: it rises to the surface before the waves carry it shoreward. A ball on the surface cannot be grabbed from the deep seabed. It slows on the sand so you can pick it up again. Reset ball returns it to the shallows. Switching characters, traveling to another destination, or carrying it out of the play area releases it where you leave it. The river and ocean beyond the buoys remain blocked.

## Characters and shapes

Zero is a small floating coral smile with a black **0** above it. It has no body cubes, eyes, or limbs. Zero can move, hop, change expression, wander, and share facts about zero.

One through Fifty each contain exactly their number of equal **1 × 1 × 1 cubes**, for **1,275 cubes** across the cast. Some original shapes are rectangles, while others have steps, extra columns, or gaps. Sixteen is a 4 × 4 square. Twenty-One is a 3 × 7 explorer carrying a modeled backpack; choose **View backpack** to inspect the camping gear and **View face** to turn back. Twenty-Seven is a true **3 × 3 × 3 cube**. Twenty-Eight is a **3 × 11 frame with five openings**, so 33 − 5 = 28.

Press **T** to cycle through factor rectangles, connected steps and other irregular forms, tens with the last digit above or beside them, and a crazy shape, then return to the original. Press **Shift + T** or the dice button for a fresh random arrangement. Every cube remains joined to the body by shared faces; no cubes overlap or change size. Two has only two orientations; Zero and One have no alternative arrangements. Cubes keep their size and colors, while the face and accessories adapt to the arrangement. Movement, collision checks, roaming routes, and camera framing use the current form. A shape that needs more room is skipped when you press T again. Zero has no blocks to rearrange, and One has only its 1 × 1 form, so their Change shape buttons are disabled.

Thirty-One has calendar rings, Thirty-Two an orange stripe, Thirty-Three a sports badge, Thirty-Four painting details, Thirty-Five rainbow flowers, Thirty-Six a superhero mask, Thirty-Seven an explorer hat, Thirty-Eight a pink side, Thirty-Nine a gray cap, and Forty a green body with patterned eyebrows. Their original cube counts match their numbers. The [reference archive](../references/characters/README.md) keeps the available user-supplied pictures.

Unselected friends wander quietly between destinations, pause, and continue exploring. Your hill, mountain and underwater visitors stay put until you move them away. Underwater friends retain their swimming height. Friends avoid scenery and each other and cross the river using bridges. Wider forms need more open space and may stay near a roomy destination.

## Feelings and math speech

Every friend has happy, excited, curious, surprised, sleepy, and sad expressions. Only the selected friend shows speech bubbles and speaks. It shares math facts periodically; press **K** or **Tell me a math fact** for another.

Facts cover arithmetic, parity, number properties, and possible arrangements. Statements about the current body follow its current shape, so a flattened Twenty-Seven does not call its body a cube. Zero teaches facts such as **5 + 0 = 5**, **5 × 0 = 0**, and **0 divided by 5 is 0**; it never claims a block shape or divides by zero.

Voices start muted. Use the sound button to enable the browser’s English speech synthesis when available. Each number has a stable combination of pitch, speaking rate and volume, and uses a recognized natural English voice. The exact sound depends on the browser’s installed voices; parameter variation still works with a single voice. Bubbles work independently of voices. Equations use spoken operator words: **2 × 10 = 20** becomes “2 times 10 equals 20.” Switching characters stops the previous voice immediately.

## New friends and bigger markets

Forty-One through Fifty join the town with their reference colors and accessories. Look for Forty-Two’s rainbow belt, Forty-Three’s open frame, Forty-Five’s staircase, Forty-Seven’s rainbow tower, Forty-Nine’s rainbow combs, and Fifty’s star eye. All 51 identities are selectable with **Tab + number**.

For numbers above 30, the first eight **T** transformations are a diamond, butterfly wings, picture frame, robot, rocket, puppy, 3D castle, and 3D bridge. Rectangles, tens-and-ones shapes, and connected crazy forms remain in the cycle. Every shape keeps the exact number and colors of its cubes.

The town now has **60 west-bank homes and 20 east-bank homes**, plus **20 stores in West Market and 10 in East Market**. Wider shop fronts, forecourts, promenades, and bridges give large friends more room. The train park is farther east beyond East Market.

Only your selected friend talks. Clear natural English voices use comfortable pitch, pace, and volume; the precise voice depends on your browser. Math remarks vary addition splits (such as 11 + 19 = 30) and explain exact subtraction or division. Meeting nearby friends occasionally starts a calculation involving both numbers, such as 30 × 5 = 150 or 30 ÷ 5 = 6. Division never uses zero as a divisor, and subtraction answers stay nonnegative.
